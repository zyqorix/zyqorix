**Scope:** the NAS sixth stack checked against BSC mainnet. Only contracts whose logic differs from mainnet were audited.
**Result:** overall **GO-with-fixes**. No Critical or High findings. One Medium (M-1) should be closed in funder before the mainnet deploy.

## Owner decisions on the findings (2026-11-06)
- **M-1: no code fix.** The overlap on the NAS came from reusing wallet addresses while testing. On mainnet the client sets separate wallets; this is checklist step 6.
- **L-2: fixed by removing `gateway.depositTo`.** It was unused and isn't planned. `depositFor` stays: it is auth-only (register), and the initial deposit at sign-up stops spam.
  - Regression test `test_depositToIsGone`.
  - Suite now 288 passing (3 depositTo tests replaced by 1).
  - Gateway is 13,288 B.
  - Gateway is a fresh deploy anyway, so this adds no mainnet step.
  - The NAS gateway 0x9AC9 still has `depositTo` until the next NAS redeploy.
- **Activation auto-pull: already in place.**
  - `activate` → `_spendWallet` (`rewards.sol:904-920`) runs `funder.pullFunding()` when the reward-credit part is larger than the pool, the same way a withdrawal does.
  - Covered by `test_activationFromCreditAutoPulls` and `test_soleMemberReactivatesFromCreditWithoutAnAdmin`.
  - No change needed.

## Follow-up 2026-11-07: withdrawal fee
- **Before:** a failed `swapAndBurnFee` was already caught, so the withdrawal never reverted. But the fee went to the funder as plain USDT, outside the reward pool. And an out-of-gas swap left only 1/64 of the gas for that fallback transfer, which could revert the whole withdrawal.
- **Now** (`gateway.sol`, owner request):
  - a fee that isn't burned is booked into the reward pool through `funder.fundRewards` (`_returnFee`);
  - a plain transfer to the funder happens only if booking fails;
  - the swap gets `gasleft() − FEE_RETURN_GAS` (150k held back) after a 450k `FEE_SWAP_GAS` floor.
- **Tests:**
  - `test_failedFeeSwapBooksTheFeeIntoThePool` covers a swap that reverts, buys nothing or runs out of gas, no position, and no liquidity;
  - `test_outOfGasSwapAtTheFloorStillPays`;
  - 6 pool expectations updated (+fee);
  - suite: 290 passing.
- **Gateway size:** 13,503 B.
- **The one fee-related revert left is `LOW_GAS_FOR_BURN`:** a transaction sent with too little gas. The app always sends at least 1.1M.

## 2. Findings (funder, gateway, rewards)

| ID | Severity | Finding | Where | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-1 | **Medium** | The trading-wallet / funding-wallet separation checks only `tradingWallet` (the disbursement contract). The 60% then moves on to the exchanger EOAs. If an exchanger EOA, or `liquidity.tradingWallet`, is also an approved funding wallet, anyone can call `pullFunding()` and book deposit money as "profit" that pays daily and generation with no trade. The deposit rule is broken by configuration, not by the code. | `funder.sol:288-296` (`addFundingWallet`); `exchangerdisbursement.sol:133` `isExchanger` | In `addFundingWallet`, also revert when `IExchanger(tradingWallet).isExchanger(wallet)`. Make "no exchanger wallet is a funding wallet" a cutover checklist item. Funder is a fresh deploy, so the fix is cheap. |
| L-2 | Low | Anyone can restart a member's 24 h withdrawal cooldown with `depositTo(victim, minDeposit)` each day. With `minDeposit = 0` it costs 1 wei. The deposit is credited to the victim, so nothing is lost, but withdrawals are blocked. | `gateway.sol:304-317`, `:550` | Restart the cooldown only when `msg.sender == user`, or keep `minDeposit > 0`. |
| L-3 | Low | The relink setters don't check that the other side points back. A one-sided `funder.setRewards`/`gateway.setRewards` makes activations and credit withdrawals revert NOT_REWARDS. `funder.setGateway` stops all withdrawals, because `rewards.gateway` is immutable. | `funder.sol:255-273`, `gateway.sol:405` | `funder.setRewards`: require `rewards.gateway() == gateway`. `funder.setGateway`: require `G.funder() == this`. |
| L-4 | Low | The vesting price is bounded below only by `floorPrice` (0.001). Once ZQR trades well above it, pushing the 30-minute TWAP down on a thin pool gives up to (market ÷ floor)× ZQR per activation. | `rewards.sol:776-799` | Admin raises `floorPrice` as the price rises. Optionally reject a TWAP far below `lastPrice`. |
| L-5 | Low | A tier added while the member is already active earns on the part-day before its activation (at most 1 day × 1% of the new tier). | `rewards.sol:762-771` | Accept it, or settle the part-day at the old value. |
| L-1 | Info (was Low) | `withdrawable()` isn't capped by the funder's USDT. A hand trace of every path shows funder USDT ≥ free balances + agent + leadership + pool, so only the `pendingFunding` term can overstate, and that reverts cleanly with nothing lost. | `rewards.sol:605-615` | Leave it. |
| I-1 | Info | Some money stays in the funder for good: leadership rounding dust, the unused referral margin, the 20% liquidity share while TOKENID is 0, and fees from failed swaps. | `rewards.sol:442,864`, `funder.sol:198-207` | Show this surplus on the Health card. |
| I-2 | Info | The 400k gas floor applies even while TOKENID is 0, when the swap is certain to be skipped. | `gateway.sol:598` | Optional. |
| I-3 | Info | No stateful invariant test of the solvency inequality. One scenario assert only. | `test/` | Add a handler-based invariant test. |

**Checked and fine:**
- `rewardPool` is credited only by `_book`, using the balance change it measures.
- Credit leaves only through `drawRewardPool`, after state is updated (CEI holds).
- USDT has no transfer hooks. A failing or out-of-gas pull is caught.
- Every loop is bounded: wallets ≤ 5, generation 10 levels, agent walk 50, leader list 50.
- `DAY` only accepts 1 day on BSC. With TOKENID 0, the swap is skipped and the fee returns to the funder. The TWAP falls back to `lastPrice`, then the floor.

**Privilege:**
- The accounts owner (an EOA) can drain the funder through a relink. This is accepted design.
- Move the owner to a multisig with a timelock on the three relinks before real deposits.
- Admins are trusted.

## 6. Mainnet cutover checklist (owner, Remix, solc 0.8.37, optimizer 200)

1. **Deploy** funder(accounts 0xAB26), then gateway(accounts, funder).
2. **Link the funder:** `funder.setGateway(gateway)`, then `funder.setTradingWallet(exchanger 0x0C2f)`.
3. **Deploy** rewards(accounts, gateway, vesting 0x6081, 86400). Then:
   - `gateway.setRewards`
   - `funder.setRewards`
   - `accounts.addAdmin(rewards)`
4. **Deploy** register(accounts, gateway). Then `accounts.addAdmin(register)`.
5. **Retire the 2026-11-03 rewards and register:** they never got admin, so check `checkIsAdmin` returns false for both. The 11-03 gateway 0x335F and funder 0xe572 are left unused.
6. **Funding wallets:**
   - `funder.addFundingWallet(w)` for each wallet, up to 5;
   - each wallet approves the funder on `/fund`;
   - **confirm none is an exchanger EOA or `liquidity.tradingWallet`** (M-1).
7. **Exchanger:** `exchanger.addExchanger(e)` for each exchanger EOA.
8. **Vesting:** send 500M ZQR to vesting 0x6081 from the supply holder.
9. **Liquidity** (FINAL contract; setup steps from the 2026-11-03 audit):
   - bind pool 0xDac4;
   - grow observation cardinality to ≥4,100;
   - mint a small seed position through a private RPC, then deposit the rest and `reinvestIdle(100)`;
   - wait 30 minutes for the TWAP.
10. **Link liquidity:** `gateway.setLiquidity(liquidity 0xb0EB)`, then `gateway.setLiquiditySlippage(300)`.
11. **Lock before real deposits:** `liquidity.lockLiquidity()`, then `gateway.lockFunder()`.
12. **Ownership:** transfer accounts ownership to a multisig.
13. **App:**
    - revert `isLocal = anvil` in `app/src/lib/appkit.js`;
    - put the new BSC addresses in `config/contracts.js`;
    - run `npm run build` (BSC) and publish with the owner's OK.
